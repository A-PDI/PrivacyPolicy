# Privacy Policy — PDI Cross-Reference Lookup

_Last updated: June 2026_

PDI Cross-Reference Lookup ("the extension") is an internal tool that helps Performance Diesel Inc. staff find the PDI part number that corresponds to an OEM or competitor diesel part number.

## What the extension does with data

**Part numbers you enter.** When you look up a part number, the extension:

- Sends that part number to the DuckDuckGo search engine (`html.duckduckgo.com`) to find interchange/cross-reference numbers, and fetches the public vendor catalog pages returned by that search in order to read their part numbers. Your query is therefore visible to DuckDuckGo and to the public websites that are fetched, subject to their own privacy practices.
- Fetches PDI's own public product catalog from `pdidiesel.com`.

**Local storage only.** The PDI catalog, the bundled cross-reference dataset, your recent lookup results, and any "not relevant" feedback you submit are stored locally on your device using the browser's `chrome.storage` API. This information is used only to make lookups fast and to let you curate results. It is **not** transmitted to the developer or to PDI servers, and it is not used for advertising.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does not collect names, emails, passwords, location, browsing history, or any personally identifiable information.
- It does not include analytics or tracking.
- It does not sell or share data with third parties, other than the search query sent to DuckDuckGo as described above.
- It does not read or modify the content of web pages you visit; it only fetches the specific catalog pages needed to resolve a part number.

## Data retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you clear it (via the extension's "Clear web cache" / "Export feedback" controls, or by removing the extension). Cached web results expire automatically after seven days.
